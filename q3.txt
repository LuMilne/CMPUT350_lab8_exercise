Q1:
    ...Which data field of your Sprite is the best candidate to move out into a separate component type in an ECS setup?...

A1:
    BasicTransform mTransform is only needed when the time comes to draw the sprite in position. A handler could manage
    the locations of sprites separately and provide the transformations for their intended locations instead.

Q2: 
    ...What does this mean we have to do if we want to render multiple, possibly translucent sprites on top of eachother?

A2: 
    We need to blend sprites in order from background to foreground, so that sprites are only tinted or obscured
    by sprites that are "in between" them and the viewer
